Icon rules

  • Interface icons: Lucide only. One stroke width, 1.75 px at 24 px, round caps and joins, currentColor. Never mix icon sets.
  • Sizes: 16, 20, 24, 32 px. Icon-only buttons keep a 44 px target and a text label for screen readers.
  • Illustrative icons: the custom 3D set (see 3D style frames), used where an icon carries meaning on its own: industries, services, process, 404.
  • Brand icons (WhatsApp and others) only from the official brand kit, used by its rules. A generic chat bubble everywhere else.
  • Never: a red cross for clinics (legally protected in India). Use a heart-pulse, stethoscope, tooth or calendar.

Photography rules

  • Subject: real Indian local businesses: a clinic reception, a gym floor, a property site visit. Commissioned, or stock with a commercial licence. Every source and licence goes in docs/ASSET_LICENSES.md before use.
  • Grade: one house grade for every photo: warm highlights, shadows that lean toward Dusk, a slight yellow warmth. A Dusk-and-yellow duotone is allowed for background photos.
  • Composite: photos sit inside phone or browser mockups with floating interface chips (“Appointment requested”, “Trial booked”, “Site visit Saturday 11:00”). Chips are interface examples and are labelled as sample data, never real customer data.
  • AI images: only for abstract textures, 3D concepts and backgrounds. Never for people presented as staff, doctors, clients or testimonial authors, and never for fake portfolio work.
  • Never: handshake, laptop-on-desk or thumbs-up stock; invented testimonials, clients or reviews.
  • Delivery: AVIF with WebP fallback, correct sizes, lazy below the fold, descriptive alt text. The hero still is the LCP element, so it is preloaded and never lazy.
