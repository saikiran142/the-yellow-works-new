Logo round 2: nine new ideas

The first five routes were thin strokes pieced together from the letters Y and W. This round starts from objects you would see on a lit shop street at night: a window, a shutter, a neon sign, an open door. Each one is a bold filled shape, so it holds up small and on a signboard.

Each route below is shown on Dusk, on Paper and in one colour on yellow, then at favicon size, as a WhatsApp avatar, as a lockup and on a lit signboard. These are concept drawings, not final vectors.

My top three: Route 7, shutter up (most Indian, most ownable) · Route 13, the lit one (tells the big idea directly) · Route 8, neon yw (best animation).
