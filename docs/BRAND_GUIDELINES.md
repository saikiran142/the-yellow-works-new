# Brand Guidelines (Phase 0 proposal — not final)

Everything here is a proposal. Phase 1 designs it, tests contrast, and asks for your choices.

## Big idea: "Lights on"
Every street has one shop that is lit up, clean and easy to find. The Yellow Works makes a business that shop online. Devices: **lit storefront** (3D signature object), **highlighter swipe** (once per screen), **spotlight glow** (the only gradient). A decorative element that can't trace back to light and visibility is cut.

## Voice
Short, commercially meaningful headlines. Write for a clinic, gym or property owner, not a developer. Plain verbs, active voice, sentence case. Buttons say what happens. Errors explain the fix, no apology. Never "innovative digital solutions".

## Logo (chosen 2026-11-23): Cube Y, "seams of light", broad Y (A2)
**Idea:** a dark isometric block with light leaking through its seams. The seams form a Y. It is "Lights on" as a single object: something closed that light breaks out of. It extrudes directly into the hero 3D cube.

**History:** three earlier sets of routes (awning monogram, YW ligature, highlighter wordmark and others; shop-themed; studio-style) were rejected by the owner. The cube was picked from a set of seven "hidden idea" concepts, then variation A, then weight A2.

**Files:** `public/brand/` (SVG source of truth, `png/` for renders). Generated by `design/phase1/build-logo.mjs` and `render-logo.mjs`. Preview: `design/phase1/logo-final.html`.

**Construction:** isometric hexagon, edge 50 on a 120-unit grid, centre (60,62). The Y arms run from the centre to three vertices (upper-left, upper-right, bottom), arm half-width 9.5 units (12 at sizes of 32 px and under). Faces: top Twilight `#2A2160`, left Dusk `#16142B`, right `#0D0C1C` on light; lifted `#3B3572 / #2A2160 / #211B4F` on dark. Y: radial gradient Butter to Highlighter Yellow to Marigold. The glow is a blurred copy of the Y.

**Variants:** full colour; on Dusk (yellow glow); Dusk on yellow; mono black; mono white (the Y is cut out, so no background is needed). Lockups: horizontal and stacked, colour, reversed and mono.

**Wordmark:** "The Yellow Works", Bricolage Grotesque 800, optical size 96, tracking -3%, sentence case. Outlined into paths by `design/phase1/outline-wordmark.py` (HarfBuzz shaping with kerning), so SVGs contain no live text.

**Clear space:** half the cube's width on every side. **Minimum size:** mark 16 px, horizontal lockup 120 px wide. Under 24 px use `mark-small.svg`; the glow mark is for 48 px and up, on dark backgrounds only.

**Misuse:** do not stretch, rotate, recolour the Y, add shadows, place full colour on clashing or busy colours, or use the glow mark on light backgrounds.

**Not done yet:** formal conflict checks (see DECISIONS D-15), the 3D GLB of the cube (part of the hero 3D work), and a check of the animated logo on a real mid-range phone.

## 3D style: "soft-touch premium toy"
Chunky rounded shapes, glossy yellow enamel hero objects, matte clay in Paper/Dusk, frosted-glass panels. Warm key light upper left, yellow/marigold rim, soft contact shadows, three-quarter camera, one shared environment. No stock 3D clichés, no photoreal humans.
Constraints: no red-cross emblem for clinics (legally protected; use heart-pulse, stethoscope, tooth or calendar). Third-party logos (WhatsApp) only from official brand kits.

## Imagery and icons
Real photography of Indian local businesses (commissioned or commercially licensed), one house grade (warm highlights, Dusk-leaning shadows). AI imagery only for abstract textures and 3D concepts, never for people presented as staff, doctors, clients or testimonial authors. Interface icons: Lucide only, 1.75 px stroke. Record every licence in `docs/ASSET_LICENSES.md` (created when the first asset lands).

## Typography (approved 2026-11-23): Bricolage Grotesque + DM Sans
Chosen over Clash Display + Satoshi (licence unchecked, would not match the logo wordmark) and Space Grotesk + DM Sans (common, reads as software). Self-host through next/font, Latin subset, `font-display: swap`. Display: Bricolage 800, optical size 96, tracking -3%; headings 700; body DM Sans 400; buttons and labels DM Sans 600. Two families only. Only the axes and weights actually used ship.

## Design tokens (Phase 1)
Source: `src/styles/tokens.css` (CSS variables as `--yw-*`, mapped to Tailwind v4 through `@theme inline`; the default Tailwind palette is removed). Covers colour, role colours, gradients, type scale, spacing (4 px base), layout, radius, elevation, motion, z-index and breakpoints.
- **Type scale** (ratio 1.25, fluid 360 to 1440 px): xs 13, sm 15 to 16, body 17 to 19, lead 19 to 24, h4 20 to 26, h3 26 to 38, h2 36 to 64, h1 46 to 96, display 48 to 136 (hero headline). Body line length 68ch. Sentence case.
- **Layout:** 12 columns, container 1280 px, gutter 16 px at 360 px growing to 32 px, sticky header 72 px with matching `scroll-padding-top`.
- **Shape and elevation:** radius 8 / 14 / 20 / 28 px and pill. Tactile press shadows (`0 4px 0` marigold or black) instead of one grey card shadow. Glow only on lit objects.
- **Motion:** 150 / 250 / 450 ms, hero 1800 ms maximum, ease-out `cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)`. Durations collapse under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Breakpoints:** 640, 768, 1024, 1280, 1536 px. Designs are drawn at 360 and 1440.
- **Verified contrast** (`design/phase1/contrast.mjs`): every pairing in section "Colour" confirmed. Muted text (Dusk at 72%) is 7.09:1 on Paper and 6.78:1 on Butter; muted text on dark (Paper at 80%) is 11.41:1 on Dusk and 9.30:1 on Twilight. Focus ring Twilight on Paper 13.78:1, yellow on Dusk 12.66:1. Dusk on Realty Cobalt is only 3.44:1, so cobalt takes white text.
- **Not yet checked:** the tokens file has not been run through Tailwind or a build (no app exists), so the `@theme inline` syntax is unverified against the installed Tailwind version.

## 3D style frames (Phase 1, 2026-11-23)
Files: `design/phase1/3d/hero-still.png` (1600 x 1000), `icon-kettlebell.png`, `icon-appointment-card.png` (900 x 900, transparent). Source: `design/phase1/three-frames.html` (Three.js), rendered by `render-3d.mjs` through headless Chrome with software WebGL.
**What the style is, as built:** chunky rounded forms, glossy enamel on the hero-colour objects and matte clay on supporting parts, warm key light from the upper left, a marigold rim light, soft contact shadow on a ground plane, one shared environment, palette colours only, no filmic tone mapping so colours stay true.
**Hero object:** a rounded dark cube with the three edges at its near corner as glowing yellow bars, seen exactly along the body diagonal, so the edges read as the logo's Y. Three floating panels in the industry accents (blank slabs; any text is added in HTML, never in 3D).
**Honest gaps:** (1) no real bloom, so "glow" is only a warm point light plus a CSS halo behind the canvas; the production R3F scene needs a bloom pass. (2) Rendered colours drift from the tokens under lighting (cobalt reads lighter than #4456FF, mint darker); the final assets need colour correction against the palette. (3) Built from code, not modelled in Blender or Spline: fine for a style frame, but production assets need proper models (GLB, Draco, KTX2) and a 3D producer (D-07). (4) The kettlebell reads as a kettlebell but its base is rough; refine before use.

## Hero animatic: lights on (Phase 1, 2026-11-23)
Prototype: `design/phase1/hero-animatic.html` (Web Animations API, replay button, final state under reduced motion). Frame strip: `design/phase1/frames/hero-animatic-strip.png`.
**Sequence, 1.8 s in total:** headline, copy and buttons are on screen from 0 s. Cube seams flicker on 0.3 to 1.0 s with a halo (0.3 to 1.3 s). Panels drift into orbit 0.7 to 1.65 s, staggered. The highlighter swipe draws behind "the one they choose." 0.85 to 1.45 s, with a Dusk copy of the text revealed in step so the phrase is never low-contrast (it is Paper on the dark stage until the swipe covers it).
**Build rules it demonstrates:** the LCP text never starts transparent; animations enhance a visible state (with no JS the swipe is visible and the text is Dusk on yellow); only opacity, transform and clip-path animate; reduced motion shows the finished frame.
**Limits:** the prototype uses the 2D logo cube with a brightness filter, not the 3D scene, and the panels are CSS. I checked frames by seeking to fixed times, not by watching it play in real time, and not on a phone. The 3D version needs the R3F scene and a bloom pass.

## Component styles, icon and photography rules (Phase 1, 2026-11-23)
Reference sheet: `design/phase1/components.html` (render: `design/phase1/frames/components.png`). Plain HTML and CSS that loads `src/styles/tokens.css` directly, so it also proves the tokens load in a browser. **Icons:** Lucide only, 1.75 px stroke at 24 px, round caps, never mixed sets; custom 3D icons for meaning-carrying spots; brand icons only from official kits; no red cross for clinics.
**Photography:** real Indian local businesses, commissioned or licensed, one house grade, composited into mockups with labelled sample-data chips, AI imagery only for abstract textures and 3D concepts, never people. No photos chosen yet (D-09).
**Not verified:** I rendered the sheet and looked at it; I did not click through the chips, phone toggle or accordion, nor tab through for keyboard behaviour. The error red `#B3261E` and success green `#0E7A5F` are new colours not yet in the token file or contrast-checked; add them as tokens before the build.

### Quality bar self-check (honest)
- *Could it be mistaken for a template?* The signature moment (glowing cube), varied section rhythm (sticky stage, bento, asymmetric list, timeline, pipeline) and the highlighter swipe argue no. Template tells I know remain: several sections open with the same left-aligned big headline plus lead paragraph.
- *One unmistakable signature moment?* Yes, the cube hero, but it is the 2D logo cube here, not the real 3D scene.
- *Does it hold up at 360 px?* Reviewed by eye at 360 px for every section except the ticker and 404. At 1440 px every section except the ticker was viewed as an isolated render; the ticker was checked in the real page top (`homepage/page-top-1440.png`). The 404 was viewed at 1440 px only. In isolated renders of the ticker and 404 the header and negative margin look wrong; that is a render-mode artifact, not seen in the real page for the ticker, and the 404 header over Dusk was not checked in the real page.
- *Premium with 3D off and reduced motion on?* Reduced motion is coded (ticker, pipeline, accordion); not tested with the OS setting on. No 3D is used in the page except two 3D icon PNGs in the industries stage.
- *Five-second clinic-owner test:* not tested with a real person.
**Known gaps:** the industries sticky pinning and tint switching are scroll-driven and could not be checked in static renders; the showcase previews are simple wireframes with labels, not full concept sites (those are Phase 4); the showcase and services use interface icons, not the planned 3D icons; no 360 px hero animation check; contrast of body copy on tinted panels (coral, cobalt) not individually re-checked beyond the token pairings.
