Seven ideas with a trick inside

A memorable logo usually hides a small idea you notice on the second look. Each of these has one: a letter hidden in a 3D form, a road that makes a choice, a W made from two beams of light.

Shown big first, then on Paper, in one colour, at favicon size, as an avatar and next to the name. Concept drawings, not final vectors.
