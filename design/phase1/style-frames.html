Three art directions

Each direction is shown as a homepage hero and the Industries section, at 1440 px wide. All three use the final logo, Bricolage Grotesque with DM Sans, and the approved palette. They differ in how the page is lit.

The hero object is the logo cube as a stand-in. Whether the hero shows the cube or the lit storefront is still open (D-16). The panel text is interface sample copy, not real customer data. Concept frames, not the build.

My pick: A, Lights on. It is the only one that uses the dark-to-light shift that the logo and the big idea are built on.

A · Lights on (recommended)

A Dusk hero where the cube glows, opening into a bright Paper page with yellow highlights and industry accents. The footer returns to Dusk.

Best at: telling the story, signature moment, premium feel. Watch out: the dark hero needs the cube to carry the page when 3D is off.

B · Own the yellow

Full-bleed yellow with oversized Dusk type. Poster-like and the loudest of the three. The cube sits on a Dusk block.

Best at: standing out in a crowd, bold first impression. Watch out: tiring over a long page, harder for a calm clinic audience, and the glowing cube loses its glow on yellow.
