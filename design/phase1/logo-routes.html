Five logo routes — rough sketches

Phase 1, step 2. Deliberately rough, drawn on a 120-unit grid with round joins. Each is shown on Dusk and yellow, then at 16 and 32 px. Not final: stroke weights, valleys and optical corrections come after you shortlist.

16 / 32 px

1 · Awning monogram

W as awning, Y as the doorway, light spilling out. Instant "local shop". Extrudes into the hero storefront. Risk: the W and Y read as two marks until the grid ties them together.

16 / 32 px

2 · YW ligature

Y and W share strokes; the stem drops from the W's first valley. Most robust at 16 px. Risk: reads as a plain W with a tail, so it carries the least story.

compact mark (swipe + YW)

3 · Highlighter wordmark

Swipe behind "Yellow"; compact version is YW on a short swipe. Easiest to animate (draw-on). Risk: type-led, so it needs the final display face; shown here in a system font. On Dusk the swipe sits behind dark text.

16 / 32 px (cone lost in mono)

4 · Spotlight Y

Y's open arms throw a cone of light; a small W stands in the beam. Best "be seen" story. Risk: the cone and the W are fine detail, and the cone vanishes in one colour, so the W is too thin at small sizes.

avatar-safe circle (rim text drops out below ~48 px)

5 · Sunburst seal

Sticker badge for avatars, invoices, merchandise. Secondary mark only. The rim text is illegible at small sizes, so it is dropped there.

Honest read: Route 2 is the safest at 16 px; Route 1 tells the best story but needs the most refinement; Route 4 is the weakest as a primary mark. Conflict checks (IP India search, reverse image) have not been run on any route yet.
